The player lets you play a video at any speed, land on the exact frame you want, and zoom in for a closer look.

## Watch and Review

The player has two modes. Switch between them with the toggle in the controls, or press <kbd>R</kbd>.

- **Review** is the default. It shows the drawing tools, comment cards, and comment markers, and stops at each comment. See [Review mode](/guides/review/).
- **Watch** plays straight through with no comments or tools.

## Play and pause

Use the Play/Pause button under the video, or press <kbd>Space</kbd>. The clock next to it shows the current time and the length of the video.

## Speed and shuttle

Choose a speed from the speed button (it shows **1×** by default): 0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 2×, or 4×.

You can also shuttle with the keyboard. <kbd>L</kbd> plays forward and <kbd>J</kbd> plays backward. Each extra press doubles the speed, up to 4×. <kbd>K</kbd> stops. Playing in reverse works too.

## Step through frames

| To | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Go back one frame | <kbd>←</kbd> |
| Go forward one frame | <kbd>→</kbd> |
| Go back five seconds | <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>←</kbd> |
| Go forward five seconds | <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> |
| Go to the beginning | <kbd>Home</kbd> |
| Go to the end | <kbd>End</kbd> |

## Zoom and pan

The zoom button shows **Fit** by default. Open it to choose **Fit**, 150%, 200%, 300%, or 400%.

From the keyboard, <kbd>=</kbd> zooms in and <kbd>-</kbd> zooms out in 25% steps, and <kbd>0</kbd> fits the video again. When you're zoomed in, drag the video to move around it.

## Volume

:::note
Added in Relua 0.3.0.
:::

Choose the **Volume** speaker button to open a slider above it. While it's open, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> change the volume, and <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump to 0 and 100. When you mute, Relua remembers your last volume while it's open.

## Full screen

Press <kbd>F</kbd> or use the full-screen button. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to leave. In full-screen Review, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> move between comments.

## The timeline

The timeline under the video is a filmstrip of thumbnails. Click or drag on it to jump to a point in the video.

In Review, it also shows comment markers. Where several comments sit close together, you see a count. Purple replay markers show [Live recordings](/guides/live/), with spans for the parts of the video each recording used.

To make the timeline area bigger or smaller, drag its edge. Double-click the edge to reset it.

## Switch videos

Click the video's name in the breadcrumb to switch to another video in the project. To step through videos from the keyboard, press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows. See [Projects, folders, and videos](/guides/library/) for more.

For every shortcut, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).
