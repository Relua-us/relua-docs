## Watch and Review

Switch with the toggle in the controls or <kbd>R</kbd>.

- **Review** (default): tools, comment cards, and markers. Stops at each comment. See [Review mode](/guides/review/).
- **Watch**: plays straight through with no comments or tools.

## Play and pause

Play/Pause button or <kbd>Space</kbd>. The clock shows the current time and the length.

## Speed and shuttle

The speed button (**1×** by default): 0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, 1×, 2×, 4×.

<kbd>L</kbd> plays forward, <kbd>J</kbd> backward. Each extra press doubles the speed, up to 4×. <kbd>K</kbd> stops.

## Step through frames

| To | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Go back one frame | <kbd>←</kbd> |
| Go forward one frame | <kbd>→</kbd> |
| Go back five seconds | <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>←</kbd> |
| Go forward five seconds | <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>→</kbd> |
| Go to the beginning | <kbd>Home</kbd> |
| Go to the end | <kbd>End</kbd> |

## Zoom and pan

The zoom button (**Fit** by default): **Fit**, 150%, 200%, 300%, 400%. <kbd>=</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> zoom in 25% steps, <kbd>0</kbd> fits. Drag a zoomed video to pan.

## Volume

The **Volume** speaker button opens a slider. While it's open, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> change the volume, and <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> jump to 0 and 100. Unmuting returns to your last volume.

## Full screen

<kbd>F</kbd> or the full-screen button. <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves.

## The timeline

A filmstrip of thumbnails. Click or drag to jump. Drag its edge to resize, double-click the edge to reset. Review adds comment and replay markers.

## Switch videos

Click the video's name in the breadcrumb, or press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows. See [Projects, folders, and videos](/guides/library/).

For every shortcut, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).
