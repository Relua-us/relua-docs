Relua works with a pen or touch screen on Windows and a stylus on a Mac. Drawing still happens in **Review** mode only. See [Draw on video](/guides/drawing/) for the tools themselves.

## Windows

### Pen

A pen draws as soon as it touches the video, and it picks up the **Pen** tool for you.

### Finger gestures

- Tap to play or pause.
- Pinch to zoom.
- Drag to move a zoomed video.

### Drawing with a finger

A finger draws only after you pick a drawing tool yourself. If you want a finger to always draw, turn on **Draw with finger**.

### Palm rejection

While the pen is at the screen, Relua ignores touches, so your hand can rest on the screen as you draw.

### Settings

Open Settings with the gear or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> and choose the **Pen & touch** tab.

- **Pen always draws**: on by default.
- **Draw with finger**: off by default.

## Mac

A stylus draws as soon as it touches the video. This works with an Apple Pencil through Sidecar, or with a graphics tablet.

Open Settings with the gear or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> and choose the **Stylus** tab. **Stylus always draws** is on by default.
