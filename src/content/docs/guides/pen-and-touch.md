Drawing works in **Review** mode only. See [Draw on video](/guides/drawing/).

## Windows

- **Pen**: draws as soon as it touches the video and picks up the **Pen** tool.
- **Finger**: tap to play or pause, pinch to zoom, drag to move a zoomed video. A finger draws only after you pick a drawing tool, unless **Draw with finger** is on.
- **Palm rejection**: touches are ignored while the pen is at the screen.

Settings (gear or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>,</kbd>) › **Pen & touch**:

- **Pen always draws**: on by default.
- **Draw with finger**: off by default.

## Mac

A stylus, such as an Apple Pencil through Sidecar or a graphics tablet, draws as soon as it touches the video. **Stylus always draws** is on by default under Settings (gear or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>,</kbd>) › **Stylus**.
