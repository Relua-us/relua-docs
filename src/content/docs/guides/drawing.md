Drawing is how you point at the exact thing you mean on a frame. Relua has a pen, an arrow, an ellipse, and a laser pointer, in five colors.

## Where you can draw

You can draw in **Review** mode only. **Watch** mode hides the tools and plays straight through. Press <kbd>R</kbd> or use the toggle in the controls to switch between them.

## The tools

The tools sit in the tool rail in the player.

| Tool | Key | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pointer** | <kbd>V</kbd> | Puts the drawing tools down so you can click around without drawing. |
| **Live** | <kbd>M</kbd> | Starts and stops a Live recording. See [Live recordings](/guides/live/). |
| **Pen** | <kbd>P</kbd> | Freehand lines. |
| **Arrow** | <kbd>A</kbd> | Straight arrows that point at something. |
| **Ellipse** | <kbd>E</kbd> | Circles and ovals around an area. |
| **Laser pointer** | <kbd>B</kbd> | A short, fading trail for pointing without leaving ink. |

Choosing the tool that is already selected takes you back to **Pointer**. <kbd>Esc</kbd> also puts the drawing tool down.

## Ink colors

When you pick a tool, the colors open beside it. There are five: Blue (the default), Red, Yellow, Green, and White.

## Undo, redo, and hiding drawings

- **Undo drawing**: <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows.
- **Redo drawing**: <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows.
- **Show / hide drawings**: <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>H</kbd>.

There is no eraser. To remove a stroke, use Undo. During a Live recording, **Clear drawings** wipes the ink on screen.

## Drawing starts a comment

Pause on the frame you want and start drawing. The first stroke pauses the video, starts a Moment comment on that exact frame, and opens the comment box. From there you can add text and post it. See [Comments and replies](/guides/comments/).

## The laser pointer

The **Laser pointer** leaves a trail that fades in under a second. It does not stay on the frame, so it is not part of a Moment. It is useful during a [Live recording](/guides/live/), which captures it as you move.

## Pen, stylus, and touch

You can also draw with a pen on Windows, or a stylus on Mac. See [Pen, stylus, and touch](/guides/pen-and-touch/).
