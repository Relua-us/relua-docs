You can draw in **Review** mode only. See [Watch and move through a video](/guides/playback/).

## The tools

| Tool | Key | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pointer** | <kbd>V</kbd> | Click without drawing. |
| **Live** | <kbd>M</kbd> | Start and stop a [Live recording](/guides/live/). |
| **Pen** | <kbd>P</kbd> | Freehand lines. |
| **Arrow** | <kbd>A</kbd> | Straight arrows. |
| **Ellipse** | <kbd>E</kbd> | Circles and ovals. |
| **Laser pointer** | <kbd>B</kbd> | A trail that fades in under a second. It is not part of a Moment, but a Live recording captures it. |

Choosing the selected tool again, or pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd>, puts the drawing tool down.

## Ink colors

Blue (the default), Red, Yellow, Green, and White. They open beside the tool you pick.

## Undo, redo, and hiding drawings

- **Undo drawing**: <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows.
- **Redo drawing**: <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> on Windows.
- **Show / hide drawings**: <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>H</kbd>.

There is no eraser. Use Undo to remove a stroke. During a Live recording, **Clear drawings** wipes the ink on screen.

## Drawing starts a comment

The first stroke pauses the video and starts a Moment on that frame. See [Comments and replies](/guides/comments/).
