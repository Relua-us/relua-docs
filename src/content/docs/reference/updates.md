## When Relua checks

At startup and every 12 hours while open, from GitHub. It is the only time Relua uses the network. A dot on the Settings gear means an update is available.

## Install an update

At the bottom of any Settings tab (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Windows):

1. Choose **Check for updates**. If you are up to date, you see **No updates available.**
2. Choose **Update**. It shows **Downloading NN%**, then **Verifying…**.
3. Choose **Restart to update** when it suits you. Relua saves drafts, unfinished drawings, and settings first.

**View release** means no automatic download fits your computer. It opens the release page.

## On a Mac

Relua must be in Applications. If you see **Move Relua to Applications and open the installed app before updating.**, move it, open it from there, and try again.

## If Relua can't reach GitHub

You see **Could not reach GitHub. Check your connection and try again.** Check that you're online, then choose **Retry**.
