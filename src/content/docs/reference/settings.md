## Open Settings

Click the gear, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Mac or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Windows. Settings has four tabs: **Profile**, **Appearance**, **Pen & touch** on Windows or **Stylus** on Mac, and **Shortcuts**.

At the bottom of every tab you find the update control and your Relua version. A dot on the gear means an update is available. See [Updates](/reference/updates/) for how updating works.

## Profile

- **Full name**: the name shown on your comments, so others recognize them. It starts as the name you typed in the **Username** dialog the first time you opened Relua.

## Appearance

Choose how Relua looks:

- **System** (default): follows your computer's light or dark setting.
- **Light**: always light.
- **Dark**: always dark.

## Pen & touch (Windows)

:::note
Added in Relua 0.3.0.
:::

- **Pen always draws** (on by default): a pen draws as soon as it touches the video and picks up the **Pen** tool.
- **Draw with finger** (off by default): when on, a finger always draws. When off, a finger taps to play and pause, pinches to zoom, and drags to move a zoomed video, and draws only after you pick a drawing tool yourself.

See [Pen, stylus, and touch](/guides/pen-and-touch/) for more.

## Stylus (Mac)

:::note
Added in Relua 0.3.0.
:::

- **Stylus always draws** (on by default): an Apple Pencil through Sidecar, or a graphics tablet, draws as soon as it touches the video.

See [Pen, stylus, and touch](/guides/pen-and-touch/) for more.

## Shortcuts

Here you can change any keyboard shortcut. The list is split into **Playback**, **Comments & drawing**, and **Library**. Use **Search shortcuts** to find one, click its key to change it, choose **Clear** to leave it **Unassigned**, or choose **Reset all** to restore every default.

See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/) for the full list and the steps.
