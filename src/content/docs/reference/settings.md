## Open Settings

Click the gear, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>,</kbd> on Windows. The update control and your version are at the bottom of every tab. See [Updates](/reference/updates/).

## Profile

- **Full name**: shown on your comments. Starts as the name you typed in the **Username** dialog.

## Account

Sign in with your email and the code we send you to use [share links](/share/links/). Your links, and who opened them, are listed here. Relua works without an account.

## Appearance

- **System** (default): follows your computer.
- **Light**
- **Dark**

## Pen & touch (Windows)

- **Pen always draws** (on by default): a pen draws as soon as it touches the video.
- **Draw with finger** (off by default): a finger always draws. Off, it draws only after you pick a drawing tool.

See [Pen, stylus, and touch](/guides/pen-and-touch/).

## Stylus (Mac)

- **Stylus always draws** (on by default): an Apple Pencil through Sidecar, or a graphics tablet, draws as soon as it touches the video.

## Shortcuts

Change, clear, or reset any shortcut. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).
