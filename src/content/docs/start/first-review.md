This walkthrough takes you through a whole review once, from adding a video to sending it to someone else. Each step links to a page with more detail.

## Walk through a review

1. **Create a project.** On the **Projects** screen, choose **New project** and give it a name. See [Projects, folders, and videos](/guides/library/).
2. **Add a video.** Inside the project, choose **Add video** and pick a video file. Relua keeps its own copy, so your original stays as it is.
3. **Find a frame.** Play the video and pause where you want to comment. Use <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> to step one frame at a time. See [Watch and move through a video](/guides/playback/).
4. **Draw on it.** Pick **Pen** (<kbd>P</kbd>) and draw on the video. Your first stroke starts a comment on that exact frame, called a Moment, and opens the comment box. See [Draw on video](/guides/drawing/).
5. **Write and post the comment.** Type in **Add a comment…** and choose **Post**, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Enter</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>Enter</kbd> on Windows. See [Comments and replies](/guides/comments/).
6. **Reply.** Choose **Reply** on the comment card, write in **Add a reply…**, and post it the same way.
7. **Play it back.** Press <kbd>Home</kbd> to go to the start, then press Play (<kbd>Space</kbd>). In **Review** mode, playback stops at your comment and shows the drawing and reply. Press Play again to carry on. See [Review mode](/guides/review/).
8. **Share it.** In the project, choose **Export**, keep **With videos** selected, and choose **Export**. Relua saves one `.zip` file you can send to anyone with Relua. See [Share a review](/share/export-import/).
