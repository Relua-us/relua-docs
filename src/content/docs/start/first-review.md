---
title: Your first review
description: Add a video, comment on it, and share it.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. **Create a project.** On **Projects**, choose **New project** and name it.
2. **Add a video.** In the project, choose **Add video** and pick a file. Relua asks **Copy the video into your Relua folder?** Choose **Copy** to keep the video with the project, or **Leave it where it is**.
3. **Find a frame.** Pause where you want to comment. <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> step one frame.
4. **Draw.** Press <kbd>P</kbd> for **Pen** and draw on the video. The first stroke starts a comment on that frame.
5. **Post.** Type in **Add a comment…** and choose **Post**.
6. **Reply.** Choose **Reply** on the comment card, type in **Add a reply…**, and post.
7. **Play it back.** Press <kbd>Home</kbd>, then <kbd>Space</kbd>. Playback stops at your comment. Press <kbd>Space</kbd> to carry on.
8. **Share it.** In the project, choose **Export**, keep **With videos**, and choose **Export**. Send the `.zip` to anyone with Relua.

More on each step: [projects and videos](/guides/library/), [playback](/guides/playback/), [drawing](/guides/drawing/), [comments](/guides/comments/), [Review mode](/guides/review/), [sharing](/share/export-import/).
