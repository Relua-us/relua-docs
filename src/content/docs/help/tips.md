These are small things that save time once you know them.

### Scrub fast with J, K, and L

Press <kbd>L</kbd> to play forward and <kbd>J</kbd> to play backward. Each extra press doubles the speed, up to 4×, and <kbd>K</kbd> stops.

### Land on the exact frame before you draw

Use <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> to step one frame at a time, then draw. Your first stroke pauses the video and pins the comment to that frame.

### Put a tool down by choosing it again

Choosing the tool that is already selected goes back to **Pointer**. You can also press <kbd>V</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

### Point at something without leaving ink

The **Laser pointer** (<kbd>B</kbd>) leaves a short trail that fades in under a second. It is handy during a Live recording, which captures it.

### See the frame clean

Press <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>H</kbd> to hide the drawings, and again to bring them back.

### Move comment cards off the action

Drag a comment card out of the way and resize it if it covers something. Relua remembers where you put it.

### Watch the clip without interruptions

Press <kbd>R</kbd> to switch to Watch. The video plays straight through with no comments, stops, or tools. Press <kbd>R</kbd> again to return to Review.

### Jump between comments

Press <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to go to the previous or next comment. In full-screen Review, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> do the same.

### Zoom in on a detail

Pick a size up to 400% from the zoom menu, or press <kbd>=</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd>, then drag to move around. Press <kbd>0</kbd> to fit the video again.

### Switch videos and projects without going back

Click the video's name in the breadcrumb to pick another video in the project, or press <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows.

To open a project directly, press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>9</kbd> on Mac, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>9</kbd> on Windows.

### Walk someone through a play at their pace

Press <kbd>M</kbd> to start a Live recording, then play, pause, scrub, zoom, and draw. The other person replays exactly what you did whenever they like. It records your actions, not your voice, so put your explanation in the comment text. See [Live recordings](/guides/live/).

### Send the videos once, then comments only

The first time you share a project, export it **With videos**. After that, **Comments only** is enough, because the other person already has the videos. See [Share a review](/share/export-import/).

### Export before you delete

Deleting a video or project removes its comments for good, and it does not go to the Trash. If you might want it back, export it first.

### Make a shortcut your own

If a default key does not suit you, change it in **Settings** › **Shortcuts**. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).
