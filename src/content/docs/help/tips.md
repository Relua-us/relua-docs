- **Scrub fast:** <kbd>L</kbd> forward, <kbd>J</kbd> backward, each press doubles speed up to 4×. <kbd>K</kbd> stops.
- **Exact frame:** step with <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, then draw. The first stroke pauses and pins the comment there.
- **Put a tool down:** choose it again, or press <kbd>V</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd>.
- **Point without ink:** **Laser pointer** (<kbd>B</kbd>) fades in under a second. A Live recording captures it.
- **Clean frame:** <kbd>Shift</kbd><kbd>H</kbd> hides and shows drawings.
- **Move comment cards:** drag or resize them. Relua remembers.
- **No interruptions:** <kbd>R</kbd> switches to Watch, without comments, stops, or tools. <kbd>R</kbd> returns to Review.
- **Jump between comments:** <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>, or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> <kbd>Page Down</kbd> in full-screen Review.
- **Zoom:** up to 400% from the zoom menu, or <kbd>=</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd>, then drag. <kbd>0</kbd> fits.
- **Switch videos:** click the video's name in the breadcrumb, or <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd><kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows.
- **Open a project:** <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>9</kbd> on Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>Ctrl</kbd><kbd>9</kbd> on Windows.
- **Walk someone through a play:** <kbd>M</kbd> starts a [Live recording](/guides/live/). It has no voice, so explain in the comment text.
- **Send videos once:** export **With videos** first, then **Comments only**. See [Share a review](/share/export-import/).
- **Export before you delete:** deleted videos and projects lose their comments for good, with no Trash.
- **Change a shortcut:** **Settings** › **Shortcuts**. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/shortcuts/).
